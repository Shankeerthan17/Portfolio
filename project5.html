Web-Based Apartment Sales System

Project Info

A web-based application designed to streamline apartment sales and management. The system allows customers to browse apartment listings, view promotions, make reservations, and track payments, while enabling staff to manage properties, customer inquiries, bookings, installment schedules, and sales reports in a centralized platform.
